Mohona Packages (BD) Limited

Trusted Packaging Partner Since 2009.
Mohona Packages (BD) Limited has spent over 15 years serving Bangladesh's export-oriented Ready-Made Garment industry. From master cartons and poly bags to footwear boxes, hanger cartons, and printed tags — we supply a comprehensive product range relied upon by 40+ international fashion brands.

Internationally Certified. Independently Verified.
We hold ISO 9001:2015, FSC, GRS, amfori BSCI, and Sedex certifications — each independently audited to the highest international standards. These accreditations give our clients full assurance over quality management, responsible sourcing, recycled content traceability, and ethical supply chain practices.

A Workplace Built on Responsibility.
We enforce a strict no child labour policy, maintain fully equipped firefighting systems on every floor, and provide free medical care for all workers. Our people-first approach reflects our belief that responsible manufacturing begins with the people behind it.

Proven Manufacturing Capabilities

Our facility produces 8,000 sq meters of master cartons, 300,000 poly bags, and 9,000 printed impressions daily — powered by German Heidelberg presses, Golder Lamination Machine, Sugiyama Whole Punch Machine, and automated poly bag lines from Taiwan and China.

Certified. Ethical. Accountable.

Our ISO 9001:2015, FSC, GRS, amfori BSCI, and Sedex certifications are backed by genuine workplace commitments — zero child labour, monthly fire safety drills, and free medical care for all staff. We don't just meet compliance standards; we build them into how we operate every day.

Proven Trade Partner for 40+ Brands

We are the preferred supplier for over 40 international fashion brands. Our 52,000 sq ft facility, powered by 130 skilled professionals, delivers export-grade packaging solutions that meet the most demanding global standards and timelines.

Products

Master Carton
8,000 sq meters/day | 7-Ply

7-ply carton production with auto and semi-auto capabilities. Engineered for strength, durability, and superior protection of garment shipments using precision Chinese Sloting & Creasing machinery.

PP & PE Poly Bags
300,000 pieces/day

Premium quality polypropylene and polyethylene bags produced on Taiwanese Blowing Machines with fully automatic side and bottom sealing. Available in multiple polymer grades for all apparel packaging needs.

Printed Corrugated Carton
7/5/3 ply | Eflute

Eflute cardboard with multi-colour custom design capabilities using Chinese Die Cutting and UV Lamination machines. Also available as Footwear Boxes, Hanger Cartons, and Photo Cards.

Packaging Components
Back Boards | Neck Boards | Collar Stands | Butterflies

Premium packaging components engineered to enhance garment presentation and protection. Produced using Chinese Creasing and Die Cutting equipment for precision and consistency throughout the supply chain.

Tags & Labels
Hang Tags | Price Tags | Swing Tickets

High quality custom printed apparel labels and hand tags using Heidelberg EINFARBEN presses. Precision colour reproduction and finishing for global fashion brands.

Offset Printing
SORSZ 9,000/hr | GTO 52-2 6,000/hr

German Heidelberg SORSZ delivers 9,000 impressions/hour, while the Printmaster GTO 52-2 runs at 6,000 impressions/hour. Complemented by a Golder Lamination Machine and Sugiyama Whole Punch Machine for precision finishing on cartons, labels, tags, and photo cards.
